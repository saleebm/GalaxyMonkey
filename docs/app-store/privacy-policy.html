Privacy Policy — Galaxy Monkey

Last updated: May 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Galaxy Monkey ("the App," "we," "us," or "our") handles your information. We built Galaxy Monkey to be privacy-respecting by design: the App does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information.

Information We Collect

None. Galaxy Monkey does not collect or process any personal data. We do not require an account, we do not ask for your name, email, or any other identifying information, and the App contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking technologies.

Data Stored on Your Device

The App saves a small amount of information locally on your device so the game works as expected:

  • Your best score.
  • Your in-game settings and preferences.

This data is stored only on your device using the operating system's standard local storage. It is never transmitted to us or to any third party. Deleting the App removes this data.

Third-Party Services

Galaxy Monkey does not integrate any third-party SDKs, advertising networks, analytics providers, or social media services. No data is shared with anyone.

Tracking

We do not track you across apps or websites, and we do not access the device advertising identifier (IDFA).

Children's Privacy

Galaxy Monkey does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. Because no data is collected, the App is safe for players of all ages with respect to privacy.

Data Security

Since the App does not collect or transmit personal information, there is no personal data for us to secure on a server. Locally stored game data is protected by your device's built-in security.

Changes to This Policy

If we ever change how the App handles data, we will update this page and revise the "Last updated" date above. Material changes will be reflected in an App update where appropriate.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at: saleebmina@copt.dev.
